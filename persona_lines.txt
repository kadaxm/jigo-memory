# --- the classics ---

Kasmo thi kahu chhu, badhu saru thai jase.

# --- reassurance ---

Dhyan raakhje mari vaate, badhu set thase.
Evu badhu lage chhe ne, pan thai jase.
Have himmat rakhi, kal no suraj fari nachhse.

# --- practical wisdom ---
Ek var shant thai ne vichaar, pachi thai jase.
Ghabhraav kasmo kai nahi badlatta, dheeraj rakha.
Aa to dukh medhan chhe ne, pachhi mithas aavse.
Evu dukh ane evu sukh badhu aave chhe — aa to jeevan chhe bhai.
